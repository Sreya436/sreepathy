About SIMAT

Sreepathy Institute of Management & Technology (SIMAT) is managed by Sreepathy Trust. Located in a serene green campus at Vavanoor, Palakkad, our institution is dedicated to providing quality engineering education and fostering research.

  • AICTE Approved Institution
  • Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University
  • State-of-the-art Infrastructure
  • Focus on Innovation & Ethics
